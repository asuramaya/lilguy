lilguy.win

Build your application profile

A profile.json of your name, contact info, and education -- optionally guessed from a resume, always reviewed by you before it leaves this page. Nothing here touches a server or an account; it's just a file you download and keep. Use it with the autofill userscript to fill out repetitive application fields on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Rippling, SmartRecruiters, and Workday.

Start from a resume or an existing profile.json

A PDF resume gets a best-effort guess at name, email, phone, school, degree, and GPA, parsed entirely in your browser via pdf.js -- never uploaded anywhere. A profile.json you downloaded before loads back in so you can edit it. Review everything below either way.
