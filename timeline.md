# Recruiting Timeline — Ops / Logistics / Supply Chain Internships

This category runs on the standard "junior year → summer before senior year"
internship calendar, but different company types post at different points in
that window. Dates are approximate and drift year to year — verify against
each company's current posting when you get there.

## The general shape of a cycle (e.g. targeting Summer 2027 as a current junior)

- **Late summer (Aug–Sep) year before**: big retail & logistics carriers
  (Amazon, Walmart, UPS) start posting first — highest volume, rolling review.
- **Fall (Sep–Nov)**: CPG and structured leadership programs (PepsiCo, GE,
  Unilever) open their cycle — these tend to have defined interview seasons
  and (sometimes) real deadlines rather than pure rolling review.
- **Fall–Winter (Oct–Jan)**: industrial manufacturers (Caterpillar, John
  Deere, Cummins, Honeywell, 3M) post on a similar but slightly later window.
- **Winter–early Spring (Jan–Mar)**: stragglers and mid-size regional
  industrials, plus a second wave for anything that didn't fill in the fall.
- **Program runs**: May/June through August (standard 10–12 week internship).

## What this means practically
- Don't wait for spring of junior year to start — by then the highest-volume,
  best-known programs (Amazon, Walmart, UPS, PepsiCo) have already closed
  their main round.
- The realistic "first wave" window to start applying is **late summer
  through fall of junior year**.
- Campus career fairs in September/October are often where these companies
  actually recruit — worth checking the school calendar against this list.
- Eligibility is commonly gated on "graduating Fall [next year] or Spring
  [year after]" — i.e. designed for a current junior — confirm per posting.
